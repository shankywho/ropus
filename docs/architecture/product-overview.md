## 1. Executive Summary

**AI Risk Manager (Ropus)** is an autonomous, real-time risk decisioning, fraud graph intelligence, and case investigation platform designed for banks, fintechs, and high-growth payment networks.

It unifies four critical defense layers into a single customer-ready operating system:
1. **Real-Time Decision Engine**: Multi-engine ensemble evaluating transactions in $<10\text{ ms}$ (Rule engine, ONNX ML models, and behavioral velocity).
2. **Fraud Graph & Threat Intelligence**: Dynamic knowledge graph tracking transnational fraud syndicates, device emulators, and money laundering rings.
3. **Autonomous AI Council**: Multi-agent deliberative layer orchestrating investigators, threat hunters, and compliance monitors with human-in-the-loop escalation.
4. **Transparent Explainability Experience**: Every decision provides a human-readable summary, factor contribution percentages, and policy triggers.
