# Real-World Fraud Scenarios & Attack Vectors

Detailed breakdown of the four canonical fraud attack scenarios simulated in ROPUS:

---

## 1. High-Value Account Takeover (ATO)
- **Vector**: Credential stuffing / session token hijacking + impossible travel velocity.
- **Indicators**: Unrecognized device fingerprint, geographic velocity ($>4,000\text{ mph}$), anomalous wire initiation.
- **ROPUS Defense**: Dynamic step-up WebAuthn MFA prompt and proactive session isolation.

---

## 2. Transnational Synthetic Identity Ring
- **Vector**: Cloned device emulators provisioning dozens of synthetic accounts with shared SSN prefixes.
- **Indicators**: Canvas fingerprint collision, shared IP proxy subnet, high clustering coefficient in Knowledge Graph.
- **ROPUS Defense**: Instant whole-community syndicate freeze across all 14 linked accounts.

---

## 3. Distributed Card Testing Botnet Storm
- **Vector**: Low-dollar ($1.00) micro-authorization flood across 50 partner merchants.
- **Indicators**: Card velocity surge (450x baseline), rotating ASN proxy pools.
- **ROPUS Defense**: Adaptive Edge DSL rule injection and 24-consortium bank indicator broadcast.

---

## 4. Multi-Hop Circular Money Laundering
- **Vector**: Smurfing deposits through 8 intermediate mule accounts before crypto exchange cashout.
- **Indicators**: High in/out velocity, circular fund topology, rapid layering within 4 minutes.
- **ROPUS Defense**: Graph Neural Network detection, settlement freeze, and automated SAR dossier generation.
